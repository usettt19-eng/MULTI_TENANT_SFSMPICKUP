01

Signing in

Same email and password every time — nothing to install, it's just a web page.

  1. Open safesmartpickup.com from a browser on your phone or the front desk computer.
  2. Enter your email and password, and tap LOG IN.
  3. No password, or forgot it? Tap No password, or forgot it? Set it here and follow the link that arrives by email.
Worth knowing

If the emailed link sends you back to the main page without opening your account, open it directly in Safari or Chrome — not from inside Gmail or the Mail app.

02

What messages you'll receive

Your account is set to receive every parent arrival notice, for any grade — not just the ones tied to your own homeroom. You'll also see Pool Day notices and replacement requests.

Everything lands in the notification bell, top right, on any screen. Titles tell them apart:

Notice titleWhat it meansWhat you do
"[Parent] arrived"A parent announced their arrival at the pickup zone.Head to the External Monitor when their turn comes up — section 3.
"Pickup via Pool Day"A different, already-authorized parent is picking up today.Just verify the driver's identity, no extra steps — section 5.
"One-day Pool Day set up" / "New Pool Day set up"A parent authorized another parent to pick up, that day or on a recurring basis.Informational — nothing to do yet.

Replacement requests (when the person picking up isn't a parent/guardian) don't arrive as a bell notification: they show up directly under Requests Center in the side menu, waiting for your review — see section 4.

  • Receiving every arrival notice adds to the dismissal staff already assigned per grade in Settings — it doesn't replace them.
  • If you should stop receiving every notice, ask the school administrator to turn it off from Staff → your profile.
03

Verify and authorize at the External Monitor

This is the screen where you confirm the adult's identity and authorize the student's release.

  1. In the side menu, open External Monitor.
  2. If your school monitors more than one gate, choose yours under Door to Monitor — the list will only show who belongs to that gate.
  3. The student at the front of the line appears on the main card, with the adult picking them up.

04

Replacements by QR: approve and scan

When the person picking up isn't a registered parent/guardian — an uncle, a nanny, a driver — the parent requests authorization from their dashboard, and you approve it before any QR code becomes valid.

  1. Open Requests Center in the side menu. You'll see the name and phone number of the person the parent wants to authorize.
  2. Review it and tap Approve or Reject. Approving generates a QR code on the parent's own dashboard — you don't create or send it yourself.
  3. The parent shares that QR with the authorized person on their own (WhatsApp, a screenshot, etc.).

When that person arrives at the gate:

  1. In the External Monitor, tap Scan Replacement.
  2. Point the camera at the QR code on their phone.

If the QR doesn't work

An expired code, one for a different student, or a request you never approved shows "Invalid QR code for replacement." Don't authorize the release without a valid QR — ask for ID and call the parent to confirm.

05

Pool Day: what those notices mean

Pool Day is when two families have already arranged, between themselves, for one parent to pick up the other's child. It doesn't go through your approval — the system just lets you know.

  • "New Pool Day set up" / "One-day Pool Day set up": a parent authorized another to pick up their child, either recurring or for a single day. Purely informational — no one is on their way yet.
  • "Pickup via Pool Day": the authorized parent is picking up right now, in place of the usual one. When they reach your line, verify their identity like you would any parent — the system already knows they're authorized.
Difference from Replacement

Pool Day is between two already-registered families (a parent picking up another parent's child) and needs no approval from you. Replacement is for someone who isn't a parent/guardian of any student — that one does need your approval first (section 4).

07

The gate the parent chose

If the school has more than one gate, the parent picks which one they're heading to before announcing arrival.

That choice shows up as a label under the student's name, both on the main card and on each row in the queue — so you know which gate to send the student to without having to ask.

08

If something looks wrong: discreet alert

If the person in front of you doesn't match the photo, or something feels off, don't let it show.

  1. Stay calm. Do not confront the individual or say anything is wrong.
  2. Tap Discreet Alert above the verification card.
  3. Contact school security through your usual channel while the system logs the event.
Protocol

The goal is to protect the student without causing a scene. Let security step in; your part is raising the alert and staying calm in front of the adult.
