01

Choose your gate on arrival

If your school monitors more than one exit gate, you can now pick which one you're heading to before announcing your arrival.

  1. On your dashboard, look for Which gate are you using? — it only shows up if your school has more than one gate.
  2. Tap the gate you'll be using.
  3. It's remembered for next time; you can change it whenever you like.
Section: C

Gate: Main Gate

Why it matters

Staff at that gate know exactly who to expect and where to send your child — no need to let anyone know separately.

02

If you can't turn on location

Some phones or browsers won't share your location with the app. That no longer leaves you stuck unable to announce your arrival — you can confirm it by hand instead.

  1. If your dashboard shows Location unavailable, you can tap Try enabling location to grant the app permission.
  2. If you'd rather not turn it on, tap I'M AT SCHOOL NOW.
  3. Confirm you're physically at the pickup area by tapping Confirm arrival.
What's different

When you confirm this way, without location, front desk sees a flag next to your name so they verify your identity a bit more carefully before releasing your child. Everything else works the same.

03

Requesting a replacement now includes a photo

If someone else — not you — is picking up your kids today, you can authorize them from your dashboard, and now attach their photo too.

  1. Tap Request Pickup Replacement.
  2. Enter their full name and WhatsApp/phone number.
  3. New: tap Take or upload a photo and add a clear photo of their face.
  4. Tap Send Request.
The photo is optional

But it makes it much easier for gate staff to recognize the person at a glance, no questions needed.

Your school's administrator reviews the request and approves or rejects it. You'll get a notification as soon as it's ready.

04

The pickup pass now shows the photo and QR together

Once approved, the replacement appears under Authorized Replacements with a share button.

  1. Tap the share icon next to their name.
  2. Your messaging app opens (WhatsApp, Messages, etc.) with the link ready to send.
  3. That person opens the link and sees their Pickup Pass: their photo on top and the QR code right below it, all on one screen ready to show at the gate.

05

Signing in: password or access link

Always the same email. Two ways in, depending on whether you have a password handy.

  • With a password: enter your email and password, tap LOG IN.
  • Without a password: tap Don't have a password? Request an access link and an email arrives to log you in directly.
  • Don't have one, or forgot it? tap No password, or forgot it? Set it here — the same link works to create a new one or change the one you had.
06

If the access link wasn't letting you in

This is now fixed: requesting the access link from your browser and then opening it from Gmail or Mail used to fail. It now works no matter where you open it.

If you still see an error

Open the link directly in Safari or Chrome (not from inside another app), or just sign in with your regular email and password.
